# About United Blockchain Society

United Blockchain Society (UBS) is an educational community focused on blockchain technology, smart contract engineering, and decentralized networks.
UBS provides an open space to explore distributed systems, acquire practical development skills, and engage with the broader Web3 ecosystem.

## Mission

To become a leading community that connects, empowers, and inspires the next generation of blockchain and Web3 builders.

## Vision

To provide builders and learners with a space to learn, build, collaborate, and connect through blockchain technology, creating meaningful opportunities to develop engineering skills and engage with the global ecosystem.

## Core Pillars

### Education

Workshops, structured reading groups, technical seminars, and self-paced modules designed to build a deep understanding of blockchain mechanics.

### Community

Spaces to meet, debate architectural trade-offs, collaborate on projects, and build long-term relationships around shared technical interests.

### Engineering

Encouraging members to translate conceptual foundations into deployed contracts, functional dApps, and open-source contributions.

### Opportunities

Connecting members with developer hackathons, competitive grants, industry conferences, internships, and technical research programs.

## Focus Areas

- Distributed ledger architectures and consensus mechanisms
- Smart contract development, testing, and security auditing
- Decentralized application (dApp) engineering
- Protocol tokenomics and economic incentive structures
- Blockchain infrastructure, validator networks, and developer tooling

## Community Scope

UBS is open to everyone interested in blockchain and decentralized technologies.
Whether exploring the space for the first time or actively writing smart contracts, all motivated learners and builders are invited to participate.
